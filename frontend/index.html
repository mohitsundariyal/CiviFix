Civic Issue Lifecycle

STAGE 01 · Intake

Report an Issue

Capture photos of road potholes, waste accumulation, unlit street lamps, or water leaks. Automatic GPS metadata places your ticket instantly onto the city engineering heat map.

STAGE 02 · Observation

Track Status

Stay informed with real-time state telemetry. Dynamic tracking indicators ensure complete transparency through every phase of public works triage.

STAGE 03 · Closure

Get It Resolved

Prompt city crew deployment, mandatory before-and-after photo verification, and citizen confirmation closure ensure issues stay solved permanently.
